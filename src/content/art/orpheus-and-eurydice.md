I chose this image after watching [*Why Do So Many Myths Say “DON'T LOOK BACK”?*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ug4NZNWS7yk). Poynter catches the story at its most fragile point: Orpheus is moving forward, Eurydice is reaching toward him, and the underworld is still visible in the landscape behind them.
