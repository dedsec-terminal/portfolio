The white sails are the first thing I can place. Two straight masts give the eye something to hold while the paint around them runs in every direction. Then a dark shape rises from the lower left. It takes a moment to see the whale, and longer still to pick out the crowded boats in the water.

Turner makes it hard to tell where the spray ends and the sky begins. The ship looks solid enough to orient the scene, then its hull almost disappears into the same pale paint. The Met notes that viewers struggled to make out the subject when the painting was first shown in 1845. There is something fitting about having to scan the canvas as if watching a rough sea. By the time the boats come into view, the patch of water around them already feels dangerous.
